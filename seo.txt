Highway Blaze 3D — Game Info (English)
=========================================

Title:
Highway Blaze 3D — Cinematic Highway Racing

Category:
Casual (Racing · Driving · 3D · Endless · Single-player · Browser · Free)

TAGS:
Casual, Racing, Driving, Highway, 3D, Endless, Near-Miss, Nitro, Traffic, Single-player, Level-Based, Browser Game, HTML5, Free-to-Play, No Download, Desktop and Mobile, Keyboard Controls, Touch Controls

Short Description:
A cinematic-real 3D highway racer. Push a 270 km/h supercar through six hand-built worlds — golden coast, sunset overpass, rainy neon downtown, snowy mountain pass, neon boulevard, and a dawn storm convoy. Score four ways: distance, near-miss combos, overspeed, and wrong-way. Chain near-misses for combo multipliers, manage the speed bomb, and thread checkpoints before the timer runs out. Free in your browser.

How to Play:
You are driving a supercar on a multi-lane highway. The road is 900 meters long and loops forever.

Steer. Four lanes in your direction. Steer speed is 18, steering acceleration 95, damping 6.5.

Speed. Min speed 12 m/s, max speed 75 m/s — that is 270 km/h. Acceleration 17.5, brake 38, coast 5.

Nitro. Tank holds 100. Drains at 30 per second, regen 9 per second, boost adds 24 m/s. You need at least 25 to fire.

Traffic. Base traffic speed is 16 m/s with ±7 variance. Cars change lanes with a 0.18 chance and signal 0.8 seconds ahead. Seven traffic colors on the road.

Score four channels:
- Distance — 0.5 points per meter.
- Near-miss — 120 base points per car passed within 3.3 meters at 88 km/h or faster. Combo window 2.5 seconds, combo bonus +25 percent per chain.
- Overspeed — 30 points per second above 190 km/h.
- Wrong-way — 55 points per second driving against traffic.

Six levels, each in its own world:
1. Golden Coast Noon — near-miss combo tutorial. Goal 1500 m. Find the gold car for a 500 bonus.
2. Overpass Sunset Dual — two-way traffic. Wrong-way bonus at 55 per second. Goal 2000 m.
3. Downtown Rain Night — wet grip at 0.72, traffic signals on. Goal 2400 m.
4. Mountain Snow Dusk — snow grip at 0.62, checkpoints every 360 meters add 9 seconds. Start timer 36 seconds. Goal 2600 m.
5. Boulevard Bomb Neon — speed bomb. Drop below 110 km/h and the fuse lights, draining 12 per second. Stay above to refill at 7 per second. Goal 3000 m.
6. Convoy Dawn Storm — two-way + bomb + wet road all at once. Goal 3600 m.

Cinematic-real rendering tier: 2048 shadow maps, fog density 0.0026, exposure 1.12, dynamic resolution down to 0.5 scale when FPS drops below 26.

Revive once per run. Three assists per run.

Free, no install, no account. Push the throttle. Thread the gap. Chain the near-miss.
